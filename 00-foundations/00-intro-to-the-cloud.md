# Intro to the Cloud
## Types of Cloud Computing
1. Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS)
    - Provide building blocks for cloud IT
    - Provides networking, computers, data storage space
    - Highest level of flexibility
    - Easy parallel with traditional on-premises IT

2. Platform as a Service (PaaS)
    - Removes the need to manage the underlying infrastructure
    - Focus on the deployment and management of your applications

3. Software as a Sevice (SaaS)
    - Completed product that is run and managed by the service provider

## Pricing of the Cloud
- Compute
    - Pay for compute time
- Storage
    - Pay for data stored in the Cloud
- Data transfer OUT of the Cloud
    - Data transfer IN is free

## How to choose an AWS Region
- **Compliance** with data data governanace and legal requirements: data never leaves a region without your explicit permission
- **Proximity** to customers: reduces latency
- **Available services** within a Region: new services and new features aren't available in every region
- **Pricing**: varies region to region and is transparent in the service pricing page

## Important Concepts
- **Cloud Computing** is on-demand availability of computer system resources, especially data storage (cloud storage) and computing power, without direct active management by the user
- **Availability Zones** are composed of one or more discrete data centers with redundant power, networking, and connectivity, and are used to deploy infrastructure.
- **Regions** are made up of a three or more Availability Zones
