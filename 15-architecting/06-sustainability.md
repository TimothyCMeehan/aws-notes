# 6. Sustainability
- focus on minimizing the environmental impacts of running cloud workloads
- Design Principles
    - Understand your impact - establish performance indicators, evaluate improvements
    - Establish sustainability goals - Set long-term goals for each workload, model return on investment (ROI)
    - Maximize utilization - Right size each workload to maximize the energy effieiency of the underlying hardware and minimize idle resources
    - Anticipate and adopt new, more efficient hardware and software offerings - and design for flexibility to adopt new technologies over time
    - Use managed services
    - Reduce the downstream impact on your cloud workloads
